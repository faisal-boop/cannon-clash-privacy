Cannon Clash — Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026 · Applies to Cannon Clash: Tiny Worlds (com.nomadarcade.cannonclash on Google Play, com.nomadarcade.cannonClash on the App Store)

The short version. You can play the whole game without an account and without giving us anything. If you choose to sign in, we store your game progress so it follows you to a new phone. We do not sell your data, and we do not show adverts in this version of the game.

Who we are

Cannon Clash is published by Alden Morris Enterprises LLC, trading as Nomad Arcade. For anything in this policy, write to support@get-creative.co.

Playing without an account

By default the game has no account. Your progress — levels, coins, diamonds, cannons and settings — is saved only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

If you choose to sign in

Signing in is optional and lives in Settings → Cloud save. It exists for one reason: so your progress survives a lost or replaced phone. You can play forever without it.

WhatWhy
Your Google account ID, name and email address (via Google Sign-In), or your Apple ID (via Sign in with Apple) on iOSTo recognise you when you sign in on another device
Your game progress: level, stars, best times, coins, diamonds, unlocked items, achievements, settings, and a record of purchases already delivered So it can be restored on a new device

This is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the United States (region us-central1, Iowa). Security rules restrict each record so that it can only be read or written by the signed-in account it belongs to.

Analytics and crash reports

ServiceWhat it receives
Google Analytics for FirebaseGameplay events only — a level started, a level finished, how long it took, which power-ups were used. If you are signed in, these are linked to your account identifier.
Firebase CrashlyticsCrash and error reports, with device model, operating system version and the state of the game when it crashed.

We use these to find levels that are unfairly hard and to fix crashes. Neither is used for advertising or sold to anyone.

Advertising

This version of Cannon Clash contains no adverts and no advertising SDK. Nothing about you is sent to an advertising network. If that changes in a future release, this policy will be updated before that version ships.

Purchases

Diamonds can be bought through the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. Those stores handle the payment — we never see your card details. We keep a record of which purchases have already been delivered, so a purchase is never paid out twice and can be restored if you reinstall.

Children

Cannon Clash is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account and its data.

Deleting your data

You are in control of all of it:

  • Delete your account and cloud save: Settings → Cloud save → Delete account and cloud save. This removes your stored progress and your account from our systems. The progress already on your phone is kept so you can carry on playing.
  • Sign out: Settings → Cloud save → Sign out. Nothing further is uploaded; your device copy is untouched.
  • Remove everything locally: uninstall the game.
  • Ask us: email support@get-creative.co and we will delete your data.

How long we keep it

Cloud saves are kept while your account exists, and deleted when you delete it. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google for up to 14 months and then removed automatically.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the data we hold, and to object to how we use it. Email support@get-creative.co and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Who else sees your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. It is processed on our behalf by Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics) and by Google Play or Apple for payments. Those companies are bound by their own privacy terms.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you in the game.
